# RouteLess AI – The Last Mile

THE SCENARIO Imagine it is an important examination day, and suddenly the rural transportation network faces an unexpected crisis: Bus breakdown: Two government buses become unavailable due to technical issues. Demand surge: Student transportation demand increases by 40%. Road blockage: A major road connecting two villages becomes inaccessible due to flooding. Time pressure: Several students have examinations scheduled within the next two hours. YOUR CHALLENGE Adapt your existing ROUTELESS solution to handle this unexpected crisis. Your system should intelligently reallocate available buses, suggest alternative routes, prioritize students appearing for examinations, and ensure that available transportation resources are used efficiently. EXPECTED OUTCOME Demonstrate a working solution that responds to the crisis, updates transportation plans, and minimizes the number of students unable to reach their institutions on time.
